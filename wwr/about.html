Rapid Roar Rafting

At Rapid Roar Rafting, our purpose is to connect people with the power and beauty of the river. Our mission is to guide every guest safely through the rapids with expert knowledge and quality equipment. Our creed is simple: respect the river, look after our guests, and keep our word. Our motto: Hear the roar. Ride the river.

History

Founded in 2004 by two adventurers of the water, Rapid Roar Rafting has grown from a small local venture into a trusted team of experienced guides.

We welcome you to join us on the river and explore the waters with us. It will be an unforgettable adventure.
